Privacy Policy

Video Factory Uploader - last updated 21 November 2026

Who uses this app

Video Factory Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google OAuth to access the owner's YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API: uploading videos, editing video titles, descriptions, thumbnails and privacy settings, posting comments, and reading channel and video statistics.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No Google user data is sent to any server operated by the app, and no data is collected from anyone other than the owner.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; locally stored tokens are then deleted.
